A HOME FOR EVERY SHOW

Your showfiles,
safely organized.

Keep your original console files in your browser’s show library. Organize them into folders by date, event, or venue, and reopen a saved show when it’s time to document it again.

  • Give every show its own folder
  • Keep originals easy to find and reopen
  • Download your files and documentation for backup
Files stay in this browser on this computer. Cloud backup and cross-device sync are not currently included.
SET THE STAGE

A stage plot generator.
A clearer plan for the crew.

Build a visual layout of your show. Place musicians, instruments, and equipment, then add the details your crew needs before load-in.

  • Place, resize, and rotate items on the stage
  • Add names, mic and DI needs, monitors, power, and notes
  • Save an editable plot or print it to PDF
Arrange your own schematic layout. Plots save in your browser and can be exported and reopened as JSON.
